While waiting for help

  • Share your exact location and a callback number with the emergency operator.
  • Follow the instructions given by the emergency operator.
  • If possible, have the patient's medicines, allergies and medical records ready.
  • Do not move someone with a suspected serious neck or spine injury unless there is immediate danger.
